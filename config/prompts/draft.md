# Final draft assembly task

You are composing two things for the issue of Tech Spark being drafted:

1. A short **editor note** (masthead) — 2-3 sentences introducing the theme
   of the month based on the stories in this issue. British, dry, confident.
2. Up to **three trend spotlights** — short synthesised paragraphs (3-4
   sentences each) drawn from clusters of stories that share a category.
   Each spotlight:
   - Has a short punchy title (under 8 words).
   - Synthesises the cluster into a single view — not a list.
   - Names what it means for energy & utilities / ScottishPower / Iberdrola.
   - Ends with a single dry aside line in the chosen tone.

You will receive the full list of ranked and annotated stories plus the
month label. Use the `submit_draft` tool to return your output.

Rules:

- Do not invent facts beyond what the stories say.
- Do not repeat headline phrasing verbatim in the editor note.
- If there are fewer than ~4 stories in a category, don't force a trend
  spotlight for it.
- Tone is controlled by `{{ tone }}` as defined in humour.md.
