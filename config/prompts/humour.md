# Tone guidance — dry_line

The `dry_line` at the end of each story is the signature Tech Spark beat.
This file documents the tone dial; the insight prompt references it via the
`tone` variable.

## dry (default)

Understated. A little arch. British. A knowing aside, not a joke. Often a
mild deflation of vendor hype or a utility-sector cliché. Never punches
down. Never self-congratulatory.

Good examples (pattern, not to be reused verbatim):
- "Another hyperscaler pledges net-zero by a date comfortably after everyone
  currently on the board will have retired."
- "Regulatory sandboxes: where innovation goes to be gently supervised."
- "A bold pledge, though the last three were also bold."

## neutral

Sober one-line observation. No humour. Still pointed — adds a view, doesn't
restate the summary. Suitable when a story is grave (major incident,
regulatory action, serious customer harm) and levity would jar.

## playful

Slightly more overt wit. Still literate, still British, still not a joke per
se. Use when a story is genuinely absurd on its own terms (vendor buzzword
excess, implausible product claims, spectacular self-own).

## Rules across all tones

- One sentence. Under ~25 words.
- No emoji. No exclamation marks. No rhetorical questions unless genuinely
  earning the beat.
- Banned filler: "unlock", "revolutionise", "game-changer", "democratise",
  "in an era of", "fast-paced world", "leveraging synergies", "at the end
  of the day".
- The line is additive — it offers a view the summary didn't state.
- Avoid punching at named individuals. Aim at claims, trends, and vendors.
